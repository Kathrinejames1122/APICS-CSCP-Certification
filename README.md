
# APICS CSCP Certification Exam Guide

[![ASCM APICS CSCP](https://img.shields.io/badge/ASCM-APICS%20CSCP-005A8B?style=for-the-badge)](https://www.ascm.org/learning-development/certifications-credentials/cscp/)
[![Credential Type](https://img.shields.io/badge/Credential-Professional%20Certification-1A3B5C?style=for-the-badge)](https://www.ascm.org/learning-development/certifications-credentials/cscp/)

A dedicated study guide and professional reference for candidates preparing for the **APICS Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) Certification Exam**, covering end-to-end supply chain management, sourcing, inventory, logistics, risk, and optimization.

---

## Exam Overview

The APICS CSCP certification validates knowledge of end-to-end supply chain management, from supplier relationships and sourcing to internal operations, distribution, and customer delivery.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based examination, available at Pearson VUE test centers and online through OnVUE.
- **Target Audience**: Supply chain managers, planners, procurement and sourcing professionals, logistics specialists, operations managers, and other supply chain practitioners.
- **Prerequisites**: No formal eligibility or experience requirement is currently required by ASCM.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Supply Chains, Demand Management, and Forecasting

- Supply chain fundamentals, objectives, and process integration.
- Demand analysis, demand patterns, and forecasting methods.
- Demand management and alignment of supply with demand.

### 2. Global Supply Chain Networks

- Supply chain network design and optimization.
- End-to-end connectivity, visibility, and coordination.
- Supply chain metrics, reporting, and network performance.

### 3. Sourcing Products and Services

- Aligning sourcing decisions with organizational demand.
- Category strategy, product design, and sourcing requirements.
- Supplier selection, contracting, and supplier management.

### 4. Internal Operations and Inventory

- Planning internal operations and production activities.
- Capacity management and production activity control.
- Inventory planning, performance, and continuous improvement.

### 5. Forward and Reverse Logistics

- Logistics and distribution processes.
- Distribution services and transportation choices.
- Trade considerations and reverse-flow activities.

### 6. Supply Chain Relationships

- Customer relationship management.
- Supplier relationship management.
- Collaboration and coordination across supply chain partners.

### 7. Supply Chain Risk

- Identifying supply chain risks and vulnerabilities.
- Risk analysis, evaluation, and prioritization.
- Developing and implementing risk response strategies.

### 8. Optimization, Sustainability, and Technology

- Supply chain strategy and tactical optimization.
- Sustainability principles and responsible supply chain practices.
- Technology trends supporting supply chain visibility and performance.
